The Raxy Labs Story

RAXY TECH LABS, a rising African tech startup and software agency, was born in Kampala from curiosity — not capital.

It started with a young technologist who didn't wait for permission, funding, or perfect conditions. What began as experimenting with code, systems, and hardware grew into a mindset: build first, learn first, improve relentlessly.

Founder

MULINDWA IBRAHIM

RAXY  ·  Raxy Tech Labs

Mulindwa Ibrahim is a self-taught technologist driven by curiosity, experimentation, and long-term vision. He began exploring software, systems, and hardware not as a business — but as a way to understand how technology really works.

Without funding or formal backing, he focused on building first — learning through trial, failure, and iteration. That mindset led to RAXY LABS — not a typical company, but a technology lab.

Key Milestones

  • › Built and delivered real-world web platforms and payment integrations
  • › Developed sell-ready systems such as POS and voting platforms
  • › Created internal prototypes in smart systems and IoT
  • › Planned long-term platforms including FX Journal and an Islamic digital platform

"Mulindwa Ibrahim is not building for quick wins. He is engineering foundations — patiently, deliberately, and with scale in mind."
